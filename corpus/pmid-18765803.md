Willcox et al. 2008, PNAS. Nested case-control of long-lived men of Japanese ancestry in the Honolulu Heart Program
against controls from the same cohort, five candidate genes. Abstract only in the cache (Europe PMC gave no open
full text); the abstract does not name the SNP.

## Reading 2026-11-11, abstract

> [q1] The OR for homozygous minor vs. homozygous major alleles between the cases and controls was 2.75 (P = 0.00009; adjusted P = 0.00135).

The candidate-gene discovery at FOXO3: homozygotes for the minor allele against those for the major, OR 2.75, P 9 ×
10⁻⁵ (1.35 × 10⁻³ adjusted). The abstract names the GG genotype but not the SNP; later work names rs2802292 G as
the protective, longevity-associated allele (pmid-41837379#q1). In Japanese Americans, by gnomAD's
East Asian frequency G is the minor allele there too (0.317, db-ensembl-116#q288). A candidate-gene result
in one cohort of men, named in row 43's prose, not counted.
