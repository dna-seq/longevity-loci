## Reading 2026-11-11, abstract

> [q1] We conducted a genome-wide association study of 1320 centenarians from the New England Centenarian Study (median age = 104 years) and 2899 unrelated controls using >9 M genetic variants imputed to the HRC panel of ~65,000 haplotypes.

The design: 1,320 NECS centenarians (median age 104) against 2,899 controls, HRC imputation. For rows 19, 31, 41,
58, 77, 92.

> [q2] The genetic associations were replicated in a genome-wide association study of 480 centenarians and ~800 controls of Ashkenazi Jewish descent.

The replication: an Ashkenazi Jewish GWAS (LGP), 480 centenarians and about 800 controls. Said of "the genetic
associations" in general; which SNPs it covers, and with what result, is in the unread main text. For row 19.

rs649357 (GRCh37 5:29,426,436), A/C: C, the coded allele (q3), in 57.92 % of centenarians' and 63.94 % of controls'
alleles, score Z −5.50, P 3.78 × 10⁻⁸, so A is the allele that goes with reaching 100 (A 0.421 in cases, 0.361 in
controls; crude odds ratio per A about 1.29, derived from the two frequencies, not printed). The table (q7) lists
every SNP at P < 5 × 10⁻⁸, several per locus at APOE; here rs649357 alone, just past the cut, although 1000 Genomes
holds 15 SNPs at r² ≥ 0.99 to it (db-ensembl-116), none listed. Their absence is informative: 5e-8 is |Z| 5.45, an
r² 1.0 proxy would carry the lead's Z 5.50 and an r² 0.99 one about 5.47, all inside the table. If the proxies sit at
A 0.31 in NECS, as in every other file read, the lead's 0.36 caps their r² near 0.8 and their Z near 4.9 (derived).
So NECS's signal rests on the lead's own imputed calls, not shown on the haplotype. Not in Supplement Table 2 (q5), so no Ashkenazi (LGP) result is read for it. The controls' A
frequency, 0.361, is above gnomAD's non-Finnish Europeans (0.30) and 1000 Genomes EUR (0.29).
rs429358 (APOE ε4's C, q8): 5.61 % of centenarians' alleles, 13.8 % of controls', Z −11.03, P 2.88 × 10⁻²⁸; crude
log odds ratio per C about −0.99, derived. Timmers 2019 rescales longevity effects to its own scale with APOE as the
empirical benchmark (pmid-30642433#q91); the same is done here, derived. For row 31.
