Biobank participants aged 60 to 70 and 10,616 TwinGene participants, meta-analysed (BOLT-LMM within each); 14 loci at

> [q6] rs61348208 is associated with parents’ lifespan (RHJ Timmers et al., 2019) and is close to significance in this analysis (RBM6 locus, p = 3*10−7)

The text puts rs61348208 at P 3 × 10⁻⁷ for frailty and labels it "RBM6 locus". RBM6 is on chromosome 3p21, not 4p16.3,
so either the label or the SNP is wrong in the paper; the value is not read as HTT's.
