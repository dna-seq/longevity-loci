Benn, Tybjærg-Hansen and Nordestgaard 2019, J Am Coll Cardiol. A genetic (Mendelian randomization) study of four PCSK9
variants, R46L among them, weighted into an allele score, against cardiovascular and all-cause death in 109,566 Danes,
validated in UK Biobank. Read at the abstract: the repository's fetch returned the abstract, cut at a "<" sign (the
Results' numbers are read in the publisher's abstract through the plugin and given here as prose).

## Reading 2026-11-14, abstract

> [q1] genotyped for PCSK9 R46L (rs11591147), R237W (rs148195424), I474V (rs562556), and E670G (rs505151).

> [q2] low LDL cholesterol due to PCSK9 variation was causally associated with low risk of cardiovascular mortality, but not with low all-cause mortality in the general population.

The design (q1): R46L (rs11591147), R237W, I474V and E670G in the Copenhagen General Population and City Heart Studies,
16,373 deaths. The verdict (q2): a PCSK9 allele score lowering LDL lowers cardiovascular death but not all-cause death.
The Results (publisher's abstract, not in the cached text): per 0.5 mmol/l lower LDL, all-cause death RR 1.01 (0.94 to
1.08) in the studies combined. Scaled to R46L's about 0.40 mmol/l (pmid-34341098#q1), 0.952 to 1.064 per allele; Timmers'
per-allele hazard ratio at the variant, 0.953 (pmid-30642433#q362), sits at that interval's edge: not excluded. An
allele-score MR, not a per-allele R46L result: named, not counted (R-TIER-4). Derived. For row 3.
