## Reading 2026-11-11, abstract

> [q1] In an additional look-up of the candidate gene FOXO3, we found that an earlier identified variant shows a highly significant association with longevity when including published data with our meta-analysis (odds ratio = 1.17; p value = 1.85×10(-10))

> [q2] did not identify new genome-wide significant associations with longevity and did not replicate earlier findings except for APOE and FOXO3.

The FOXO3 P of 1.85 × 10⁻¹⁰ (OR 1.17) is a candidate look-up pooled with published data (q1), not a genome-wide
result of the GWAS itself, which found nothing new at genome-wide significance (q2). Deelen's table names the variant
rs2802292, effect allele G, with 10,574 cases (pmid-31413261#q106), more than the GWAS's 6,036: the published
candidate-gene samples (Willcox's, Flachsbart's and others, unnamed in the abstract) are inside it, so it is a
meta-analysis of the discovery and its replications, not an independent test of either. Timmers 2020 says FOXO3 had
"never" reached genome-wide significance (pmid-32678081#q21, q22), which reads the same way. For row 43.
