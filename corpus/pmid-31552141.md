Rosa et al. 2019, NPJ Genomic Medicine. Cis-Mendelian randomisation of plasma soluble IL6 receptor (sIL6R, a
negative regulator of IL-6 signalling) on cardiovascular, immune and longevity outcomes, with 34 independent cis
variants within 250 kb of IL6R as instruments; longevity as parents' age at death in UK Biobank (the Neale
laboratory's summary statistics). Read from Europe PMC's open full text (PMC6754413).

## Reading 2026-11-11, full_text

> [q1] Genetically-determined reduced IL6 signaling lowers the risk of multiple CVDs and is associated with increased longevity, but at the expense of higher atopic risk.

The abstract's conclusion: less IL-6 signalling (more sIL6R) lowers several cardiovascular diseases and goes with a
longer life, and raises atopic disease.

> [q2] The lead variant in this region is rs4129267 (PGWAS = 7.41 x 10−1101), which is in perfect LD (r2 = 1) with the non-synonymous variant rs2228145.

The sIL6R GWAS lead, rs4129267, is in perfect LD with rs2228145 (row 8's lead).

> [q3] Fathers age at death 248,726 34 0.007 0.004 0.010 3.83 × 10−5

> [q5] Mothers age at death 199,690 34 0.008 0.004 0.012 2.11 × 10−5

Table 1, longevity: genetically higher sIL6R lengthens fathers' (β 0.007, P 3.8 × 10⁻⁵) and mothers' (0.008, P 2.1 ×
10⁻⁵) age at death, inverse-variance weighted over 34 instruments, n 248,726 and 199,690. In UK Biobank, the same
parents as Timmers 2019's UK Biobank part.

> [q4] There was no IV located in exon of IL6R.

No instrument is rs2228145 itself: the MR result is for the IL6R locus's soluble-receptor level as a whole, not a
per-allele result at the variant. Its link to rs2228145-C rests on the catalog (C raises sIL6R,
db-gwascatalog-2026.10.29#q35-q37) and on q2: derived. An annotation-class MR result (R-TIER-4: never A or B). For
row 8.

> [q6] Additional sensitivity analyses were performed by removing the lead variant associated with sIL6R.

> [q7] fathers age at death (Beta: 0.008; 95% CI: 0.003–0.012; Pcausal = 4.69 × 10−4) and mothers age at death (Beta: 0.011; 95% CI: 0.006–0.016; Pcausal = 5.19 × 10−6)

A correction to the paragraph above: the MR does not leave the variant out. The lead instrument rs4129267 is in perfect
LD with rs2228145 (q2), and q4 says only that no instrument is exonic. Rosa ran the MR again without rs4129267, and
parental age at death stayed significant: fathers beta 0.008, P 4.7 × 10⁻⁴; mothers 0.011, P 5.2 × 10⁻⁶ (q6, q7). For
row 8.
