## Reading 2026-11-16, full_text

> [q1] Here we quantify mtDNA copy number (mtCN) and heteroplasmy using blood-derived whole-genome sequences from 274,832 individuals and perform genome-wide association studies to identify associated nuclear loci.

> [q2] Our genome-wide association study (GWAS) identified 92 linkage disequilibrium (LD)-independent nucDNA association signals across 46 loci (Fig. 1d)

> [q3] The nuclear loci we identify, including those with fine-mapped missense variation (for example, MGME1, POLG, POLG2, DGUOK, LONP1), are enriched for roles in the mtDNA nucleoid, mtDNA replication and nucleotide balance.

mtDNA copy number from 274,832 whole genomes (q1); 92 independent nuclear signals at 46 loci for adjusted copy number
(q2); LONP1 among the loci with fine-mapped missense variation (q3). For row 106 (D32, D37.12).
