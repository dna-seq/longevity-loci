Kashtanova et al. 2026, Aging and Disease (CC BY, the publisher's PDF through Unpaywall, cached by `fetch-supp` as
this note's supplement text). Whole-genome sequencing of 3,703 Russian adults aged 90 and over against 22,354 aged
18 to 75; a longevity GWAS (the long-lived against the population sample) and a "healthy longevity" GWAS within the
long-lived, each cross-validated over nine splits whose P values are averaged geometrically.

APOE ε4's C, OR 0.67 in the long-lived, P 4.8e-10 (q3, Table 1). Every other line is short of genome-wide: CFAP46's
rs61863089 T OR 0.82, P 5.7e-7 (q4), with APOE's rs769449 and APOC1's two at 1e-7 to 4e-7 (Table 1, ε4's shadow);
the healthy-longevity GWAS's best are MYO18B's rs73168992 T OR 11.2, P 1.6e-7 (q5), a TBC1D28 deletion at 4.5e-7
(q6), and LOC105376454's rs76682565 at 9.0e-7 (q7), none replicated and all in the same sample. So no new locus here
