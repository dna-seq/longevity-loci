## Reading 2026-11-17, supplement

> [q1] We fine-mapped six independent HLA-II haplotypes in 6,053 individuals (443 cognitively healthy centenarians [CHCs], 3,219 population controls, 2,391 AD patients).

> [q2] Hap-R (DRB1*01:01) increased AD risk and reduced healthy longevity, with nominally reduced microglial HLA-II activation.

> [q3] Hap-Y (DRB1*15:01) showed increased microglial HLA-II activation in post-mortem brain tissue, independent of quantitative AD neuropathology.
