> [q1] Our final GWAS identified two loci that achieved genome-wide significance: one within the APOE region (lead SNV: rs429358) and another within the EYS gene (lead SNV: rs75571981, Fig. 2a, Supplementary Table 2).

> [q2] In this study, a GWAS of 964 Japanese centenarians (including 173 supercentenarians) and 7,306 controls was conducted

> [q3] Genomic DNA was isolated from 1,027 Japanese centenarians and 30,081 controls (Supplementary Fig. 1). After applying quality control and exclusion criteria, the final analysis included 964 centenarians and 7,306 controls for primary GWAS and 60 centenarians and 10,000 controls for polygenic score studies.

> [q4] Notably, this variant has not been identified in other East Asian populations, including the Korean population.

> [q5] An interesting characteristic of this SNV is that while the MAF showed no significant change in healthy agers compared to controls, it exhibited a notable increase, specifically in centenarians.

> [q6] Multivariate Cox proportional hazard analyses incorporating multiple genetic factors (sex, APOE4 allele, EYS allele, and CentPGS) demonstrated the significant association of APOE4 and sex with lifespan, whereas APOE4, sex, and CentPGS were significantly associated with DFS (Fig. 6c).

> [q7] However, neither existing expression databases nor predictive algorithms (Enformers) for quantitative expression trait loci have identified a clear functional consequence of this variant.
