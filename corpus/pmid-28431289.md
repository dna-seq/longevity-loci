## Reading 2026-11-12, abstract

> [q1] In this study we show that, relative to noncarriers, KL-VS heterozygotes had (1) shorter survival; (2) smaller white matter volumes; (3) slower cognitive decline; and (4) greater right frontal lobe volumes.

> [q2] The KL-VS heterozygote survival and white matter volume disadvantages were unexpected.
