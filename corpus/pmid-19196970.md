## Reading 2026-11-11, abstract

> [q1] The FOXO3A association was considerably stronger in centenarians than in nonagenarians, highlighting the importance of centenarians for genetic longevity research.

> [q2] Our findings confirmed the initial discovery in the Japanese sample and indicate FOXO3A as a susceptibility gene for prolonged survival in humans.

The first replication of Willcox's FOXO3A association, in Germans of both sexes (q2), stronger in centenarians than
nonagenarians (q1), a hint the effect grows at the oldest ages. Candidate-gene, one population: named in row 43's
prose, not counted.
