Deelen et al. 2014, Human Molecular Genetics. A longevity GWAS meta-analysis in Europeans: 7,729 cases aged 85 or
more (5,406 of them 90 or more) against 16,121 controls under 65, from 14 studies in 7 countries, then a replication
of the top SNPs in 6 further studies (13,060 cases, 7,330 of them 90 or more, against 61,156 controls), joint
analysis of both phases, a prospective survival meta-analysis in 11 cohorts, and look-ups in blood-pressure,
coronary and metabolic GWAS. Read from Europe PMC's open full text (PMC4103672). Supplementary Tables S1 (cohorts),
S5 (prospective cohorts) and S10 (blood-pressure adjustment) are not read: Europe PMC's supplementary bundle answered

> [q5] Although the association of this SNP with survival up to 85 years is not genome-wide significant (OR = 1.07, P = 4.34 × 10−6; Table 1), the locus likely affects survival from middle age onwards.

> [q6] we performed a subset analysis in which we compared cases aged ≥90 years (n = 5406) with 15 112 controls (<65 years) from the corresponding control cohorts. Replication was performed in 13 060 cases aged ≥85 years (of which 7330 were ≥90 years) and 61 156 controls from 6 additional studies

> [q7] Some cohorts only provided controls (GOYA, NTR, SU.VI.MAX, TwinsUK and WTCCC2) or only cases (BELFAST, CEPH centenarian cohort, Danish longevity study I/II, Leiden 85-plus Study I/II and Newcastle 85+ Study), whereas others contained both (Calabria cohort, deCODE, EGCUT, GEHA Study, German longevity study, Leiden Longevity Study, Rotterdam Study I/II and TwinGene).

> [q8] P-values <5 × 10−8 were considered genome-wide significant

> [q9] we performed a prospective meta-analysis of the 2 SNPs in 34 103 individuals aged 30–105 years from 11 different cohorts, of which 8582 had died after a mean follow-up time ranging from 2.2 to 17.4 years

> [q10] carriers of the minor allele of rs2149954 (T) demonstrated significantly lower all-cause mortality (HR = 0.95, P = 0.003; Supplementary Material, Table S6)

> [q11] Carriers of the minor allele of rs2149954 have a lower mortality risk for cardiovascular disease (CVD) (HR = 0.86, P = 0.004), which mainly appeared to be caused by protection from stroke (HR = 0.60, P = 2.27 × 10−7). In addition, we observed an effect of this SNP on non-CVD mortality (HR = 0.86, P = 0.002)

> [q12] showed a significant association of rs2149954 with a decreased risk for coronary artery disease (CAD) (OR = 0.96, P = 0.011)

> [q13] showed a significant association of the minor allele with lower diastolic (P = 3.46 × 10−5) and systolic (P = 6.55 × 10−6) blood pressure

> [q14] its association with decreased all-cause mortality was not influenced by blood pressure in two studies of participants aged ≥75 years (PROSPER and Leiden 85-plus study Cohort II; Supplementary Material, Table S10)

> [q15] located in an intergenic region on chromosome 5q33.3, 302 kb downstream of the EBF1 gene

> [q16] None of the SNPs showed an association with gene expression in the various examined tissues

> [q17] replication in additional cohorts from European as well as non-European descent is warranted
