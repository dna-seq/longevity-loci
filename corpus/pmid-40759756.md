Foote et al. 2025, Nature Genetics. Genomic SEM over frailty-index deficits: a general frailty factor and six
residual factors, one of them "multimorbidity" (factor 3). Inputs are consortium GWAS where available, otherwise the
Pan-UK Biobank's summary statistics, so its sample overlaps UK Biobank (Zenin 2019's). Europe PMC's open full text is
read; the per-locus tables are not, but the factor 3 deposit is (row 51).

## Reading 2026-11-11, full_text

> [q1] the multimorbidity factor (defined by the number of illnesses and high mean arterial pressure) is a strong driver of frailty over and above the variance captured by a general aggregate measure of all frailty deficits

> [q2] The effect estimates for each trait were formatted in a direction that reflected the ‘risk-inducing’ phenotype for frailty

> [q3] When consortia data were not available, we used GWAS summary statistics downloaded from the Pan-UK Biobank

Factor 3 is defined in part by high mean arterial pressure (q1), and effects point to the frailty-raising side (q2):
a negative β is less frailty. Its set at 4p14 (Open Targets, db-opentargets-26.09#q422-q425) colocalises with the
blood-pressure sets at H4 about 0.98, so at row 27 it is read as the blood-pressure signal carried into a frailty
factor, supporting healthspan but not separate evidence of it. UK Biobank enters through the Pan-UK Biobank inputs
(q3). For row 27.
