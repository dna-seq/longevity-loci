> [q1] Here, we show that overexpression of Klotho in mice extends life span.

> [q2] Klotho protein functions as a circulating hormone that binds to a cell-surface receptor and represses intracellular signals of insulin and insulin-like growth factor 1 (IGF1), an evolutionarily conserved mechanism for extending life span.
