Row 148, rs6543176 (SLC9A2 intron). The Long Life Family Study's whole genomes: 568 extreme-longevity cases (past the
age fewer than 1 % of their sex and birth cohort reach) and 908 controls, spouses and others who died before that age
(q1, q2, q13); a mixed-effects logistic model with saddle-point approximation, sex, four principal components and the full
relationship matrix. A, the rare allele, reaches genome-wide significance, −log10 P 8.3 (q3), at MAF 0.01 in the cases
against gnomAD's 0.006 (q4, q14); no effect size or control frequency is printed in the text. 24 of the 587 families carry
it (q12); the cohort's MAF is 0.007 (q11). Replication failed for want of a usable line: imputation in the New England
Centenarian Study fell under Rsq 0.7 (q5, q15) and UK Biobank's parental-longevity GWAS does not hold it (q6). The portals'
signs are recoded to the rare allele (q7): UK Biobank's PheWAS has A with lymphoid leukaemia, OR 9.03, P 0.00016 (q8),
and with less hypertension, OR 0.92, P 0.13 (q9); in LLFS A carriers have less hypertension, HR 0.62, P 0.08 (q10).
