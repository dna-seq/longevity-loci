## Reading 2026-11-14, abstract

> [q1] rs2273500-C, a splice site acceptor variant resulting in an alternate CHRNA4 transcript predicted to be targeted for nonsense-mediated decay, was associated with decreased CHRNA4 expression in physiologically normal human brains

> [q2] likely through its effect on smoking, as rs2273500-C was no longer associated with lung cancer after adjustment for smoking.

Row 99: C, the minor allele, raises nicotine dependence (P 8.0e-9 across the samples, OR 1.12 for severe against
mild dependence), changes a splice acceptor so that an alternate CHRNA4 transcript is made, one predicted for
nonsense-mediated decay, and lowers CHRNA4 expression in normal brain (q1). C raised lung cancer risk (OR 1.06), and
no longer did after adjustment for smoking (q2): the lung effect runs through smoking. For row 99.
