Beccacece et al. 2026, GeroScience. A genome-wide association study of longevity in 705 people from Calabria,
Southern Italy, long-lived cases against younger controls, with a mixed model for relatedness and population
structure; the GWAS Catalog's record (GCST90726611, db-gwascatalog-2026.10.29#q393) gives 299 cases and 406 controls
from the ECHA and GEHA cohorts. Read from the abstract (Europe PMC) and the publisher's two supplementary files (the
Excel workbook, Tables S1 to S9, and the PDF of Supplementary Figures 1 to 3). The main text is not read: the journal
version is not open, the CC BY preprint (bioRxiv, v1, 2025-12-19, an earlier title without "exploratory" and three
fewer authors) answers 403 to fetch-supp, and Europe PMC holds the preprint (PPR1230815) without its text. So the
case and control ages, the replication cohort's size, design and overlap with the discovery, and the model behind
each Beta are unread here.

## Reading 2026-11-12, abstract

> [q1] we performed a genome-wide association study of longevity in 705 Calabrian individuals, comparing long-lived subjects to younger controls using a mixed-model approach that accounts for relatedness and population structure.

The design: 705 Calabrians, long-lived against younger, a mixed model. For row 62.

> [q2] We identified 22 suggestive risk loci, among which one reached genome-wide significance.

One locus genome-wide; by Table S6's order (q12), rs11186068 is it, the only line under 5e-8. For row 62.

> [q3] Although most loci did not replicate in external datasets, an intronic variant regulating proteasome-related gene expression was confirmed by meta-analysis.

The authors' own summary of replication: most loci do not replicate externally; the one confirmed by meta-analysis
is an intronic variant tied to proteasome-related expression; the authors annotate rs11186068 as intergenic (Table
S1, q7) and Table S7 (the eQTL pairs) has no line for it, so it is not that variant. Which variant it is, and what
the other external datasets showed for the lead, is in the unread main text. For row 62.

> [q4] Notably, established longevity loci such as APOE and FOXO3 were not associated, underscoring population-specific genetic effects.

APOE and FOXO3 are not associated in this sample (Table S5, q13). For row 62.

> [q5] Overall, our preliminary findings suggest that longevity in the Calabrian population arises from a combination of unique genetic influences and conserved aging-related mechanisms, providing new insights into the molecular basis of human lifespan extension.

The authors call the findings preliminary; the journal title calls the study exploratory. For row 62.
