Yashin et al. 2015, Frontiers in Genetics 6:122. A GWAS of lifespan in Framingham Heart Study original-cohort women
(EMMAX, Affymetrix 500K and 50K, a "modified" control for population stratification), confirmed in the cohort's men and
in the offspring cohort, then Cox models of survival past 80 and of CVD and cancer. The source of the GWAS Catalog's
five lines (TLK2, ECHS1, NRDE2, RIMBP2, CYP51A1). Read from Europe PMC's open full text (PMC4394697) and its
supplement (Presentation1.PDF, through Europe PMC's supplementaryFiles service).
