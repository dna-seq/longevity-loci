Zhang and colleagues 2025, BMC Cardiovasc Disord. Drug-target variants, R46L for PCSK9 inhibition, against
cardiovascular outcomes after a diagnosis, in a longitudinal health-record cohort, with propensity matching.

## Reading 2026-11-14, full_text

> [q1] The LoF variant rs11591147 (p.Arg46Leu) represents the effect of PCSK9 inhibition as it disrupts the function of the PCSK9 which results in higher amount of cell surface LDL receptor (LDLR) and internalization of LDL, leading to lower serum LDL levels and subsequently confer protection against ischemic heart disease (IHD) [11–13].

> [q2] After matching, a genetic proxy for PCSK9 inhibition (rs11591147) improved survival from CV death/heart transplant in individuals following a diagnosis of ischemic heart disease (Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.78, P = 0.03).

R46L is loss-of-function: it disrupts PCSK9, raising cell-surface LDL receptor and lowering LDL (q1). After an
ischaemic heart disease diagnosis, in matched European-ancestry patients, T carriers reached cardiovascular death or
heart transplant later (HR 0.78, P 0.03; unmatched 0.95, not significant) (q2): a disease cohort and a cardiovascular
endpoint, not a lifespan-class test. Named. For row 3.
