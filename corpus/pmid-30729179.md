Zenin et al. 2019, Communications Biology. GWAS of healthspan, the hazard of the first of the common age-related
diseases or death, in 300,447 genomically British UK Biobank participants; replication in 96,313 other UK Biobank
participants. A positive β is a higher hazard, an earlier end of healthspan. Read from Europe PMC's open full text
(PMC6353874).

## Reading 2026-11-11, full_text

> [q11] HLA-DQB1 is significantly (p = 4.18 × 10−8) associated with COPD, diabetes, cancer and dementia in this study and was demonstrated to be associated with parental survival earlier in ref. 13.

The discussion, on the multimorbidity GWAS: the HLA-DQB1 locus (rs1049053's, Table 2) is associated with COPD,
diabetes, cancer and dementia together. Zenin reads it as part of a possible genetic signature of ageing, a
locus acting across diseases. For row 103.

> [q12] For replication, we used a combination of the UK Biobank participants not included in the discovery set that comprised rest of European ancestry individuals (self-reported white, data-field 21000, n = 81,099)

> [q13] Additionally, we checked that there is no individuals with kinship coefficient r > 0.01 between discovery and replication cohorts, using relationship data provided by UKB (UKB data category 100315).

The replication sample (N 96,313) is other UK Biobank participants, mostly self-reported white Europeans outside
the discovery set (which also left out UK BiLEVE's participants), checked for no kinship with it: independent individuals from the same biobank. rs1049053 is not replicated there
(β 0.013, P 0.146, the same side; q7). For row 103.
