Zenin et al. 2019, Communications Biology. GWAS of healthspan, the hazard of the first of the common age-related
diseases or death, in 300,447 genomically British UK Biobank participants; replication in 96,313 other UK Biobank
participants. A positive β is a higher hazard, an earlier end of healthspan. Read from Europe PMC's open full text
(PMC6353874).
