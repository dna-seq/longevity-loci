> [q3] We found that the genetic variant rs2069837 in IL-6 was protective against critical COVID-19 (combined p = 4.64 × 10−16, OR = 0.49).

> [q4] When analyzing the effect of this variant on the IL-6 in the serum based on the rs2069837 genotype, we found that the A to G variation in rs2069837 decreased the expression of IL-6, especially in the male.

> [q5] An in vitro assay showed that the A to G allele changes in rs2069837 within IL-6 obviously decreased the luciferase expression activity.
