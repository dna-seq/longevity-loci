## Reading 2026-11-11, abstract

> [q1] identified genetic susceptibility loci for Takayasu arteritis with a genome-wide level of significance in IL6 (rs2069837) (odds ratio [OR] 2.07, P = 6.70 × 10(-9)), RPS9/LILRB3 (rs11666543) (OR 1.65, P = 2.34 × 10(-8)), and an intergenic locus on chromosome 21q22 (rs2836878) (OR 1.79, P = 3.62 × 10(-10)).

The lead is genome-wide for Takayasu arteritis (OR 2.07, P 6.7e-9). The abstract does not name the risk allele and the
GWAS Catalog records it as "?" (risk frequency 0.91, which fits A in Turks and Europeans but is not a statement of
the allele). Its direction is unknown here. For row 48.
