Wissel et al. 2026, European Journal of Preventive Cardiology. IL6R p.Asp358Ala against chronic disease and survival
to 90 in the Women's Health Initiative: 38,807 genotyped postmenopausal women, 23,464 of them old enough to reach 90
by February 2023, 12,181 of whom did; median follow-up 20 years. Multi-ethnic; the abstract gives no ancestry
breakdown. Read from the abstract (not in PMC).

## Reading 2026-11-11, abstract

> [q1] Carrier status of the IL6R variant (rs8192284; p.Asp358Ala) was determined via genotyping.

The paper genotypes rs8192284, merged into rs2228145 (db-ensembl-116#q7-q8): seed locus 157's rsID.

> [q2] No significant difference in the likelihood of surviving to age 90 was detected between women with 2 alleles of the IL6R gene variant compared to women without any allele (Odds Ratio, 1.00; 95% confidence interval, 0.91-1.09).

Survival to 90: two copies against none, OR 1.00 (95 % CI 0.91–1.09); a genotype contrast, not a per-allele
estimate, with no allele named (the variant allele is the minor C, 358Ala). Null. The interval does not exclude an
effect the size of Timmers 2019's (pmid-30642433#q35) or Wright 2019's: a null without power to contradict them,
not a contradiction (derived).

> [q3] IL6R blockade was not associated with incident chronic-disease risk, including invasive cancer and longevity, in a large, ethnically diverse cohort of postmenopausal women.

No association with coronary heart disease, heart failure, stroke or invasive cancer either. For row 8.
