replication look at Longchamps 2022's variants. Europe PMC's open full text and the supplementary tables (mmc2).

## Reading 2026-11-16, full_text

> [q1] We carried out genome-wide and phenome-wide association studies (GWASs and PheWASs, respectively) separately in two sub-cohorts with largely African (N = 8,598) and European (N = 22,068) ancestry.

> [q2] We estimated mtCN in peripheral blood using exome sequence data, taking cell composition into account.

> [q3] These studies were largely based on the same dataset (i.e., the UK Biobank), so we restricted our analysis to variants identified in Longchamps

> [q4] Of these, only three were significant at a replication threshold of 4.5×10−4 (0.05/110) in the EUR cohort: rs3110823 in the gene STMP1 (βAallele=0.056,p=2.24×10−06), rs10419397 near the gene USHBP1 βAallele=0.047,p=8.69×10−7, and rs12247015 βAallele=0.039,p=1.14×10−5 in the 5′ UTR of TFAM.
