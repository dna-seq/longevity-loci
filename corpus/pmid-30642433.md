the 26 LifeGen cohorts, UK Biobank lives removed from LifeGen), Cox model; effects are years of life per copy of
the lifespan-increasing allele in self (the parental estimate doubled). Read from Europe PMC's open full text
(PMC6333444); the age-band and replication source data are eLife supplements the cache does not hold.

> [q7] Strikingly, two of the three largest LVE loci for non-lung cancers (at or near ATXN2/BRAP and CDKN2B-AS1) show increased cancer protection associating with decreased lifespan (due to antagonistic pleiotropy with CVD), while the third (at or near MAGI3) also shows evidence of pleiotropy, having an association with CVD three times as strong as breast cancer, and in the same direction.

The trade-off at CDKN2B-AS1 and ATXN2/BRAP: the alleles that protect against non-lung cancers go with shorter
lives, because the same alleles raise cardiovascular disease. So the lifespan-raising allele carries more
non-lung cancer risk, and the cardiovascular side wins on lifespan.
