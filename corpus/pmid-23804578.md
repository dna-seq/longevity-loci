Garagnani et al. 2013, Aging (Albany NY). 31 type 2 diabetes SNPs genotyped in 562 diabetic patients, 558
non-diabetic controls matched to them for age, sex and origin, and 229 centenarians, all from Northern and Central
Italy. Read from Europe PMC's open full text. The paper prints allele frequencies per group and tests diabetics
against each control group; it prints no centenarian-against-control test, so any such number here is derived.

## Reading 2026-11-12, full_text

> [q1] A total of 1,349 individuals from Northern/Central Italy, including 562 T2D patients (D) (mean age: 65.76 ± 8.11) and 558 unrelated age-, gender- and geographically-matched controls (CTR) (mean age: 58.11 ± 12.40), and 229 centenarians (100+) (mean age: 105.0 ± 2.9) have been considered in this study.

The design: 562 diabetics (mean 65.8), 558 matched non-diabetic controls (mean 58.1), 229 centenarians (mean
105.0), Northern and Central Italy. For row 63.

Diabetics against centenarians: T in 31.4 % of the centenarians' alleles. Centenarians against the non-diabetic
controls (derived, D29.5, as at row 31; not printed): crude OR per T (0.314/0.686)/(0.370/0.630) = 0.78, log OR
−0.249 (SE 0.118 from 458 and 1,116 alleles), one-sided P 0.017 (allelic z 2.11): T, the diabetes risk allele, is
rarer among centenarians than among middle-aged non-diabetic controls. The controls are selected free of diabetes,
which lowers their T and so works against this contrast. Overlap with Deelen 2019's GEHA Italian arm unknown (both
collect Italian centenarians; neither paper lists the other's subjects). For row 63, rs7903146, r² 1.00 to the
