Park et al. 2025, Nature Communications. A GWAS of own survival in UK Biobank's European-ancestry participants
(393,833 people, 35,551 of them dead; 180,970 men, 21,059 dead), imputed array variants down to MAF 0.1 %, with
Martingale residuals of a Cox model as the outcome (BOLT-LMM), for the whole cohort and for each sex; and gene-based
rare-variant tests in the exomes. Its own look-ups in FinnGen (r11, "Death") and in LifeGen with UK Biobank
(parental age at death) are the paper's validation; the second shares UK Biobank with the discovery. Read from Europe PMC's open full text (PMC11871019) and the Supplementary Information PDF from the
publisher's site; CC BY 4.0. Several numbers sit inside typeset formulas the cached text keeps as LaTeX, so a quote
stops where one begins and the number beside it is said in the prose.
