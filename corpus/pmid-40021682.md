Park et al. 2025, Nature Communications. A GWAS of own survival in UK Biobank's European-ancestry participants
(393,833 people, 35,551 of them dead; 180,970 men, 21,059 dead), imputed array variants down to MAF 0.1 %, with
Martingale residuals of a Cox model as the outcome (BOLT-LMM), for the whole cohort and for each sex; and gene-based
rare-variant tests in the exomes. Its own look-ups in FinnGen (r11, "Death") and in LifeGen with UK Biobank
(parental age at death) are not independent of each other's people, and the second shares UK Biobank with the
discovery. Read from Europe PMC's open full text (PMC11871019) and the Supplementary Information PDF from the
publisher's site; CC BY 4.0. Several numbers sit inside typeset formulas the cached text keeps as LaTeX, so a quote
stops where one begins and the number beside it is said in the prose.

## Reading 2026-11-11, full_text

> [q1] using Martingale residuals on 393,833 individuals including 35,551 deceased subjects (mean age at death: 71.2 years) and 358,282 living subjects

The whole-cohort GWAS: 393,833 people, 35,551 of them dead.

> [q2] Two loci reached genome-wide significance (GWS)

> [q3] on chromosomes 19 and 6 (Fig. 1A). On chromosome 19, rs429358 was the lead variant at the APOE locus

In the whole cohort only two loci pass the threshold, APOE (chromosome 19) and ZSCAN23 (chromosome 6): PRKD3 is not
genome-wide there.

> [q4] In sex-stratified GWAS (180,970 males and 212,863 females), the APOE locus was again linked to reduced lifespan in both males and females

The sex-stratified GWAS: 180,970 men and 212,863 women.

> [q5] In males, a significant association with reduced lifespan was observed for rs577106756_A located in intron of PRKD3 on chromosome 2 (β = 0.09, p = 3.2

rs577106756 (PRKD3, intron), in men only: A goes with reduced lifespan, β 0.09 on the Martingale residual, P
3.2 × 10⁻⁸ (the exponent is in a formula the quote stops before). A positive β is more deaths than expected, a
shorter life: the authors' own reading of this β.

> [q6] MAF = 0.1%). PheWAS analysis, based on the UKB Neale v1 dataset, revealed that rs577106756_A was associated with ICD10 code C10.9, Malignant neoplasm of oropharynx, unspecified, as the primary cause of death

> [q7] ×10−8, OR = 1.04), and self-reported “Stomach Cancer”

The authors' PheWAS of rs577106756's A in UK Biobank (Neale v1): oropharyngeal cancer (ICD-10 C10.9) as the
primary cause of death, P 4.5 × 10⁻⁸, OR 1.04, and self-reported stomach cancer, P 6.1 × 10⁻⁷, OR 1.003. The
same UK Biobank people, and the cause-of-death record among the same deaths that make the lifespan signal: not an
independent trait. MAF 0.1 % in the GWAS, at the analysis's own frequency floor.

> [q8] However, neither rs577106756 nor rs547541271 showed a significant association with the “Death” phenotype in FinnGen (p = 0.55 and 0.93, respectively) (Supplementary Table 2).

FinnGen's "Death" phenotype does not replicate rs577106756: P 0.55 (Supplementary Table 2: β −0.07 for A, q15).

> [q9] Similarly, rs35705950 showed a significant association with increased death in FinnGen

On FinnGen's scale a positive β is increased death (rs35705950's T, β 0.034 in Supplementary Table 2, q16), so
rs577106756's A at −0.07 leans the other way from the discovery. FinnGen's test is both sexes, a binary outcome;
its power for a 0.1 % variant is not stated.

> [q10] with lifespan for the entire cohort, as well as stratified by sex. For all three analyses, we used Martingale residuals calculated using the Cox proportional hazards model as the outcome variable.

Three GWAS: the whole cohort, men and women; Martingale residuals of a Cox model the outcome.

> [q11] Genome-wide significance threshold was set at the standard GWAS level

The paper's significance call: 5.0 × 10⁻⁸ (in a formula), for each of the three GWAS, with no further correction
for running three.

> [q12] While our study lacks an independent external replication

The authors' own statement that the study has no independent external replication.
