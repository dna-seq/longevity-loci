Zeng et al. 2016, Scientific Reports. A GWAS of Han Chinese centenarians against middle-aged controls from the Chinese
Longitudinal Healthy Longevity Survey (CLHLS), in two regional datasets (south, north) used as discovery and
evaluation for each other, then combined. GWAS Catalog GCST003425. Read from Europe PMC's open full text
(PMC4766491) and the supplementary PDF from its supplementary archive.

## Reading 2026-11-11, full_text

> [q1] We identified 11 independent loci associated with longevity replicated in Southern-Northern regions of China, including two novel loci (rs2069837-IL6; rs2440012-ANKRD20A9P) with genome-wide significance and the rest with suggestive significance (P < 3.65 × 10−5).

> [q2] Finally, after various stages of sample filtering, 2,178 centenarians (mean age 102.7 ± 3.49 (SD)) and 2,299 middle-age controls (mean age 48.4 ± 7.44 (SD)) were included in the subsequent GWAS dataset (Supplementary Table 1).

> [q3] The Southern region dataset consists of 1,063 centenarians and 887 middle-age controls and the Northern region dataset consists of 1,115 centenarians and 1,412 middle-age controls (Supplementary Table 1).

The design: centenarians against middle-aged controls (mean 48), R-TIER-2's old-against-young class.

> [q7] Similarly, our identified SNP rs2069837 has a MAF of 0.14 in Asian populations but 0.09 in the European population, indicating that the previously reported SNP rs2069827 is a European-specific longevity associated genotype and our identified SNP rs2069837 could be a Han Chinese-specific longevity associated genotype.

The authors' own reference frequency for G in Asians is 0.14; their southern controls carry 0.033 (q4) and the
combined controls 0.095. 1000 Genomes' southern Han Chinese (CHS) carry 0.143 and northern (CHB) 0.160
(db-ensembl-116#q323, q324): the southern controls are about a quarter of the reference, the northern ones close to

the discovery as an overfit source on these (§4.1's D, as row 41). For row 48.
