## Reading 2026-11-11, full_text

> [q1] We identified 11 independent loci associated with longevity replicated in Southern-Northern regions of China, including two novel loci (rs2069837-IL6; rs2440012-ANKRD20A9P) with genome-wide significance and the rest with suggestive significance (P < 3.65 × 10−5).

> [q2] Finally, after various stages of sample filtering, 2,178 centenarians (mean age 102.7 ± 3.49 (SD)) and 2,299 middle-age controls (mean age 48.4 ± 7.44 (SD)) were included in the subsequent GWAS dataset (Supplementary Table 1).

> [q3] The Southern region dataset consists of 1,063 centenarians and 887 middle-age controls and the Northern region dataset consists of 1,115 centenarians and 1,412 middle-age controls (Supplementary Table 1).
