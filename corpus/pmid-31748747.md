## Reading 2026-11-16, full_text

> [q1] This was applied to 205,011 men from UKBB (aged 40-70) in whom we identified 41,791 (20%) with detectable LOY.

> [q2] To confirm the validity of our identified signals we sought replication in three independent datasets.

> [q3] Firstly, we used data generated using 653,019 male research participants from the personal genetics company 23andMe, Inc. (Table S1).

> [q4] These samples differed from the discovery samples both in terms of DNA source (saliva rather than peripheral blood) and LOY measurement type (quantitative mLRR-Y rather than dichotomous PAR-LOY calls).

> [q5] Third, we replicated our loci in a set of 95,380 Japanese ancestry men from the BBJ project, with LOY estimated using mLRR-Y in whole blood.

Loss of Y called as a yes/no trait (PAR-LOY) in 205,011 UK Biobank men, 20 % with detectable loss (q1); replication in
three independent data sets (q2): 653,019 men of 23andMe, saliva and quantitative mLRR-Y (q3, q4), deCODE, and 95,380
Japanese men of Biobank Japan (q5).
