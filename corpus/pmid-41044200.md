## Reading 2026-11-16, full_text

> [q1] Genome-wide analyses in up to 56,348 individuals unveiled a heritability of 23–29% attributable to common genetic variants and highlighted 59 associated loci (39 novel).

> [q2] We begin by discovering new loci in a sample of 32,634 individuals of White British ancestry and replicate our findings in a multi-ancestry sample of up to 23,714 individuals.

> [q3] Genetic association analyses were performed on BAG, that is, the difference between brain-predicted and chronological age.
