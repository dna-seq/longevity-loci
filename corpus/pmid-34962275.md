## Reading 2026-11-16, abstract

> [q1] I performed a GWAS on blood DNA methylation (DNAm) levels of 2309 Taiwan Biobank (TWB) participants.

> [q2] Owing to the fact that the sample size of an individual GWAS of DNAm data is still not large, I adopted the 'prioritized subset analysis' (PSA) method to boost the power of a GWAS.

> [q3] Moreover, I identified five novel loci, including rs117530284 that was associated with the 'epigenetic age acceleration' (EAA) according to Lu et al.'s GrimAge (called 'GrimEAA').

> [q4] each 'A' allele of rs117530284 in the IBA57 gene was found to be associated with a 1.5943-year GrimEAA (95% confidence interval = [1.0748, 2.1138]).

2,309 Taiwan Biobank participants (q1), a prioritized subset analysis to boost power (q2); rs117530284 a new GrimAge
acceleration locus (q3): each A adds 1.59 years of GrimAge acceleration (1.07 to 2.11), with sex, BMI, smoking,
drinking, exercise, education and ten ancestry components as covariates (q4). For row 112 (D32, D37.12).
