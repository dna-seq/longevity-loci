Akiyama et al. 2023, Communications Biology (PMC9898503). A genome-wide association study of survival time after
registration in the BioBank Japan project: 137,693 Japanese patients of a hospital-based biobank, 31,324 of whom died
during a mean follow-up of 7.44 years, analysed by sex with a Cox model adjusted for age, sex, ten principal
components and the diseases each person had at baseline, then meta-analysed. Read from Europe PMC's open full text and
the publisher's supplementary files: the Supplementary Information (PDF, Supplementary Figures and Tables 1 to 7),
Supplementary Data 1 (the sensitivity analysis by disease) and Supplementary Data 2 (the previously reported loci,
with effect and other alleles). The GWAS Catalog holds the study as GCST90270909 without full summary statistics.
