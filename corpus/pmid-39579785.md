Hamilton et al. 2025, The Lancet Microbe. rs2228145 against tuberculosis in a meta-analysis of 17 GWAS (19,302
cases, 1,019,821 controls, several ancestries), with MR on CRP and IL6R protein. Read from the abstract (not in PMC).

## Reading 2026-11-11, abstract

> [q1] including the non-synonymous variant, rs2228145, for which the C allele contributes to reduced classic (cis) IL-6 signalling activity

> [q2] For each additional rs2228145-C allele, the odds of tuberculosis reduced (odds ratio [OR] 0·94 [95% CI 0·92-0·97]; p=6·8 × 10-6).

C is the reduced-signalling allele; each C lowers the odds of tuberculosis (OR 0.94, P 6.8 × 10⁻⁶), across
ancestries. The abstract adds that C's effects in critical COVID-19, Crohn's disease and rheumatoid arthritis are
of the same direction. For row 8: an infection the drug class is feared to raise is lowered by the variant.
